# OpenFUT20 Local - Changelog

## V30 - Player Pick Stability, Special Portraits & Gold Players Pack

### Player Picks
- Improved Player Pick stability and reward handling.
- Fixed selection flow issues that could cause crashes when confirming a Player Pick.
- Improved handling of custom and special-card Player Pick rewards.
- Added safer fallback behavior for Player Pick portraits.

### Special Card Portraits
- Fixed incorrect portrait aliasing affecting non-Icon special cards.
- Improved portrait resolution for TOTY, TOTS, TOTW, TOTW Moments, TOTY Nominee and other special-card rarities.
- Special cards now prefer their correct card-specific portrait when available.
- Icon Player Picks retain same-player portrait fallback behavior when required.

### Packs
- Added the Gold Players Pack.
- Contains 12 Gold players only.
- Guarantees at least 1 Rare Gold player.
- Excludes Bronze, Silver and special-card items from this pack.

### General
- Improved compatibility between Player Picks, pack rewards and card presentation.
- Additional stability and cleanup for local Ultimate Team gameplay.
